How this works: Identify 2–3 recurring microaggressions you’ve faced in leadership spaces. For each, name what happened and its impact, choose a response strategy, and write the response you’d actually use. The power isn’t in the perfect comeback — it’s in choosing on purpose.
NAME IT
Address it directly, in the moment.
ASK A QUESTION
Make them explain it, calmly.
ADDRESS LATER
Raise it privately — or document & escalate a serious pattern.
LET IT PASS
Decide, on purpose, it isn’t worth your energy today.
