Scenario Practice
The Meeting That Talks Past Itself

You're in a student organization meeting. An older advisor and a first-year member keep misfiring. Every time the first-year speaks, the advisor hears someone disengaged — vague, distracted, not that invested. Every time the advisor responds, the first-year hears someone dismissive — talked down to, not taken seriously.

Here's the part they can't see: they actually want the same outcome. They just can't hear each other through the difference in how they each show up. And you are in the room.

Apply → ROOT LISTENING & COMMUNICATING ACROSS DIFFERENCE
