How this works: Choose a goal you care about, then map the people around it. Tag each as an ally, accomplice, or bystander — then pick two you could move one step closer, and name your first ask. You can’t move the room alone.

ACCOMPLICE
Takes real risk with you. Acts and shares the cost.
BYSTANDER
Neutral, watching. Not against you — not yet with you.
