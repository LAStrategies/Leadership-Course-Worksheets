Room-Reading

Read the room — power, energy, voice — then choose the mode it needs.

✏️ Activity S — Situational Leadership ⏱ 20 min
Your Progress0%
How this works: For each room, first read it — power, energy, and voice — then choose the leadership mode it needs and name your first move. There are no wrong answers; the practice is in reading before you react.
READ · POWER
Who holds formal authority — and who holds informal influence?
READ · ENERGY
Skeptical, tense, excited, checked-out? What can the room handle?
READ · VOICE
Who’s talking, who’s quiet, who wasn’t invited in?
THEN CHOOSE
Direct · Coach · Support · Delegate — the mode the moment needs.
