Reflection Journal
Your name, date, and cohort are saved at the top of every page. Fill them in above and they will carry through the whole journal.

Write after each lesson. Be honest, not polished. This is a tool for you, not a performance for anyone else. The more you put in, the more useful it becomes when you need it most. There are no wrong answers here — only honest ones.

How this journal works
  • Use the sidebar to navigate to any lesson's reflection prompts.
  • Your responses are saved automatically in your browser as you type.

  • Revisit your responses — especially at Module 6 — to see how you have grown.
