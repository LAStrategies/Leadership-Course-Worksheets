Values Card Sort

Your values have names — let's find them.

Becoming the Leader Only You Can Be  |  Rooted Leaders RISE  |  Life Aligned Center for Leadership & Justice Studies  |  Module 2, Lesson 2 · 25 min

How it works: Work through all three rounds in order. In Round 1, drag each value into the pile that feels right. In Round 2, choose your Top 5. In Round 3, name your Core 3 and complete your Values Map. Go with your gut — your first instinct is often the most honest answer.

Reflection Questions

JOURNAL
1
Which 3–5 values feel most essential to who you are as a leader?
2
How do these values show up — or get suppressed — in different contexts (school, work, family, community)?
3
Who taught you each value? Family, community, an experience, or hard necessity?
4
Where do you feel the most alignment between your values and your actions? The most tension?
💬 Discussion Prompt

Share your top 3 values and one specific story of how you've lived one of them in action. What made that moment feel like leadership — even if no one called it that?
